Storage runs

To the unit, from the unit, between units.

Self-storage, garage clear-outs, loft-to-ground, house-to-container — we shift the lot. One van, booked by the hour or the job.

Call 07479 222 460 Get a quote

When storage runs come up

  • House move with a gap. Completing Friday but new place isn't ready till Tuesday? We shift to storage, come back and finish.
  • Renovation. Big kitchen job, whole-room clear-outs — into storage, back out when you're ready.
  • Downsizing. Parents moving to a bungalow or flat — we sort what goes to storage, what goes to the kids, what goes to clearance.
  • Student storage. Summer break, cheaper to store than ship home.
  • Estate & probate. Clearing a house that needs sorting — we can run pieces to relatives, storage, or auction house.

Who we run to

Big Yellow, Safestore, Ready Steady Store, Access Self Storage — all the chains have units we've been in and out of dozens of times, up and down the A565 between Crosby and Southport.

How it works

Call Leigh on 07479 222 460. Tell him where it's coming from, where it's going, roughly how much stuff (single room, garage, whole house), and the access at both ends. Honest quote on the call — fixed price or by the hour, whatever suits. Most single-run jobs are £80–£180.
