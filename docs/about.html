About Leigh

One bloke, one van, one reputation.

Lemovals is run by Leigh Dacosta Greaves — born and still based in Crosby. No office, no call centre, no layers of admin between you and the man with the van.

Why Lemovals exists

Leigh started out doing removals the old-fashioned way: mates' houses, neighbours' flats, friend-of-a-friend jobs. Word got round. A few years in, Lemovals was a proper business — serving the L22, L23, and L30 streets first, then across the wider Merseyside area.

The reason people recommend Leigh isn't that he's the cheapest or the biggest. It's that he turns up when he says he will, quotes a fair price and sticks to it, and takes care of your stuff like it's his own. Simple as that.

What makes it different

  • Same person, start to finish. Quote, planning, turn-up, drive, unload — Leigh's there for all of it. No surprise replacement team, no hand-off to a subcontractor.
  • Fully insured. Goods in Transit and Public Liability. Your stuff is covered; your house and the house you're moving into are too.
  • Environment Agency registered for waste carriage — so clearance work is done legally and properly.
  • Crew when we need one. For bigger jobs Leigh brings a second lad (sometimes two) — trusted mates he's worked with for years. Not a gig-economy stranger.

The kit

A Luton-bodied van with a tail-lift for the bulk of jobs. A smaller SWB van for tight-access streets and single-item work. Moving blankets, straps, trolleys, sack barrows, piano dollies, the lot. Not rented-for-the-day kit — proper gear that does the job properly.

Where we're based

Crosby, L23 — a short hop from the heart of the home patch. Ten minutes to Waterloo, fifteen to Bootle, twenty to Walton. If you're in Merseyside, we're never far.
