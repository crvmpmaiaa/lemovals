The Maghull patch

L31 is classic north Merseyside semi-detached territory — long avenues of 1960s and 1970s brick semis with driveways, garages, and reasonably wide estate roads. The station quarter around Maghull railway station has a mix of post-war terraces and some older Victorian stock closer to the town centre. Melling and the Lydiate fringes add rural outliers into the mix.

It's good-value family housing and there's a lot of it — which means Lemovals picks up plenty of work here when families upsize or downsize across the postcode. Properties are typically well-maintained with proper hallways and staircases, which makes the job straightforward.

Access notes

  • Most estate roads in Maghull are wide enough for the luton van — no awkward squeezing past parked cars on the main drags.
  • Town centre terraces near Station Road have on-street parking only; early starts help avoid the school-run crunch.
  • Some closes off Hall Lane and Damfield Lane are tight at the entrance — Leigh will assess on the day and bring the smaller van if needed.
  • Melling properties can involve farm tracks or unmade roads; worth mentioning when you call.
