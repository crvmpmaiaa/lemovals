House & flat moves

Your whole move, handled.

From a one-bed flat in Waterloo to a full five-bed in Formby — Leigh and the crew pack it, load it, drive it, and put it where you want it.

Call 07479 222 460 Get a quote

What's included

  • Proper padding. Moving blankets on every piece of furniture — not just sofas, everything.
  • Dismantle & reassemble. Wardrobes, beds, dining tables. We take them apart, we put them back.
  • Packing option. Boxes, tape, bubble wrap — we'll pack a few fragile rooms or the whole house, your choice.
  • Heavy stuff. Pianos, safes, white goods — no extra fuss, we've done thousands.
  • Access anywhere. Third-floor walk-up? No parking? Narrow stairs? Tell us — we plan around it.
  • Rubbish clear. Left-behind bits go in the van and get disposed of properly, if you want.

How it actually goes

You call Leigh on 07479 222 460 — or fill in the quote form and he'll ring you back. He'll ask where you're going, how many rooms, any pianos or tricky access, and your preferred date. Quote is honest, usually within a fiver or tenner of the final job.

On the day we turn up when we said we would, crack on, and get you keys-in-the-door. No hidden fees, no "oh and you also owe us for…" surprises. Finished jobs get paid cash, card on the van, or bank transfer before we leave.

Typical jobs this month

  • One-bed flat, Waterloo L22 → two-bed terrace, Crosby L23. Half a day. One van.
  • Three-bed semi, Thornton → downsize to bungalow, Formby. Full day. Two vans.
  • Student room, Kensington → family home, Crosby. A couple of hours. Van and one lad.
  • Four-bed detached, Blundellsands → Chester. All-day, two vans, overnight job with storage.

Pricing

Honest answer: it depends on size, distance, access and what you want packed. Local moves in the L22/L23/L30 home patch start around £200–£350 for a small flat, £400–£700 for a house. Wider Merseyside and longer distances go up from there. Leigh gives you a fixed price on the call — no surprise hourly clocks running.
